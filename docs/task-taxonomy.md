# Embedded-software business task taxonomy

The visible taxonomy is intentionally business-oriented rather than benchmark-oriented.

## Scope

The study starts at the **software boundary**. System requirements and customer documents may be inputs, but upstream system-engineering work itself is out of scope.

The taxonomy covers:

1. Software requirements & traceability
2. Architecture & detailed design
3. Embedded development
4. Debug, integration & upstream
5. Verification, reviews & quality
6. Safety, cybersecurity & process
7. Build, CI, tooling & release
8. Documentation & knowledge capitalization
9. Project, meetings & engineering platforms
10. Research, technology watch & continuous learning

Each task defines:

- business definition;
- inclusions/exclusions;
- examples;
- Definition of Done;
- sector applicability: Common / Automotive / Industrial IoT;
- tools/platforms;
- formats/artifacts;
- actions expected from the AI solution;
- standards/technology context;
- typical data sensitivity.

## Enterprise environment represented

The taxonomy explicitly models GitLab/GitLab CI, Jira, Confluence, SharePoint, Reqtify, OpenFastTrace/Sphinx, Microsoft Office formats, text-as-code documentation, HIL benches, embedded debug/programming tools and common static-analysis/test frameworks.

Automotive and Industrial IoT are modeled mostly as **sector tags and workflow variants**, avoiding unnecessary duplication of tasks.
